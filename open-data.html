The file

What is
withheld.

Behind these figures is one row per dispatch transmission captured on the monitored talkgroups, with the call type, the severity, the responding unit and the time it went out. That record is not published.

It is worth being exact about why. Everything on this page is an aggregate count. The underlying record is row level and located to the street, and row level street located dispatch data is a different kind of disclosure from a count, no matter how careful the counts are. Radio transcripts, full addresses, coordinates and anything that identifies a member are not in it either, and have never been published anywhere on this site.

What we do publish is the method. The scripts that build these figures are readable, so how a number is computed can be checked even though the rows behind it cannot be refetched.
