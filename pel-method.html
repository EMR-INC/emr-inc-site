Event load · how a class is decided

A word is
not a finding.

So what is it?

EMR Inc. is building a measure of the events a firefighter has actually been at: not a prediction, not a diagnosis and not a wellness score. This page is the method behind it. It shows the 8 event classes, the 76 radio terms that can open a candidate for review, the published sources behind each class, and, at length, what those sources say they do not support.

Read this first

Nothing on this page is scored, and nothing here is validated. The term list is version pel-keywords-v3-proposed, authored 2026-10-06, and no clinical reviewer has read it. A matched word opens a candidate for a person to review. It does not establish that an event happened, it does not reach a member's record, and there is no threshold for it to cross. 5 of the 8 classes rest on a single empirical paper each once the definition they all share is set aside.

01 · The route

Every path ends where the evidence stops.

A term matching in a radio transcript is the weakest thing in this system, and the drawing says so. Follow any line to its end and it terminates at a stated fact, at a refusal, or at a gate that is closed because nobody has been appointed to open it. There is no line from a word to a score, because there is no score.

22 of the 76 terms are words the research itself names as insufficient on their own: a Mayday, a mass casualty declaration, a pediatric dispatch, a resuscitation in progress. They are in the list so that a reviewer sees what was said. They carry no weight.

02 · The classes

Eight classes, each with a boundary written before the terms.

Each class states what it requires before it can attach to anything, and what is explicitly not enough. The second column is the one that does the work: it is where the research declines to let a dispatch code stand in for an experience.

Two classes have no terms, on purpose

A victim the member knew known_victim. Private only. Searching for relationship language would infer the relationship the library forbids inferring. Reaches a record only by voluntary private confirmation.

Repeated exposure across many incidents cumulative_aversive_detail. Repetition across incidents, which one narrative cannot show. The library carries no validated threshold and forbids creating a synthetic incident to represent a pattern.

03 · The evidence, drawn

One source is on all eight classes, and it is a definition.

Every class cites the same official guidance page on what a traumatic stressor is. That page is a definition, not a finding. Set it aside and the picture changes: 5 of the 8 classes rest on one empirical paper each. The 21 terms about a member trapped or out of air stand on one source: 164 Australian firefighters, questionnaire study, 2000.

That is not a reason to stop. It is the reason the page exists: a reader can see the width of the evidence before anybody quotes a number off it.

04 · In the library's own words

The research declines what a metric most wants.

These are quotations from the limitation column of the source records, not our paraphrase of them. Each one rules out a move this kind of build naturally makes.

Event weights

PC02, 2000: “No validation of entrapment trigger or event weights; no personality profiling.” Seven of the eight classes carry “No default weight” as an explicit column. Any weight in this system is a guess and is labelled one.

Dispatch classification

PC05, 2013: “Does not validate a fixed half-life, automatic dispatch classification or symptom inference.” So a dispatch code does not classify an experience, and a decaying count with a fixed half life is not supported either.

A dose curve

PC01, 1999: “Supports event and context documentation, not a universal event-to-symptom dose curve.” It is also the one source in the set whose finding is mixed rather than an association.

A route from an incident flag

PC07, official agency guidance: “Incident flags alone cannot establish a personal Criterion A route or PTSD.” That sentence is the whole argument of this page, written by somebody other than us.

And the gap nobody has closed

All 6 empirical sources concern events, exposure routes and populations. None of them concerns language in radio traffic or in a patient care record. A source supporting “being trapped is associated with posttraumatic symptoms” does not support “the phrase low on air in a transcript indicates it.” That is the real evidential gap, it is the first thing a clinical reviewer should press on, and it is why a matched term produces a candidate rather than a count.

05 · The list

Every term, and how it is counted.

Published in full. A member whose radio traffic could be read by this system is entitled to see the whole list rather than a sample of it, and a reviewer cannot rule on a list they have to ask for.

How terms count

Terms stack. More terms matching means more things were true about the call, so a specific term adds to a general one rather than replacing it. The one exception is two words for a single declaration: terms sharing a fact group describe one fact and are counted once between them. 76 terms resolve to 21 fact groups plus the ungrouped singles.

Radio dialect

2 terms are signal codes rather than plain English. A code means different things in different agencies, so a single global code list would create facts the agency never said. A dialect term matches only where its department has confirmed the code, and no department has confirmed one, so these are off everywhere today.

The source that is in the library and cited by nothing

PC08 is in the library and no class cites it. US Department of Veterans Affairs, National Center for PTSD. PTSD Checklist for DSM-5 (PCL-5). Describes a 20-item self-report assessment and clinician interpretation; screening is not a dispatch-derived diagnosis.

The rolling count of events has no threshold and no instrument behind it, and an outside instrument is the intended route to validating a figure. This is a candidate for that role and not part of the measure. Its own limitation sets the shape of any such use: “No questionnaire values, cutoff flags or inferred symptoms in shared incident or unit records.” It may validate a figure. It may never feed one.

PC09 through PC21 exist in the library and are not recorded here. No event class cites any of them. Read them before relying on the library beyond the keyword chain.

08 · The scope

Five phases, and four of them are gates.

The work is staged so that each phase has to be approved before the next one can begin. Any automated alert, clinical interpretation or referral trigger stays disabled until its method, validation, authority, consent, access controls and response workflow are each separately approved. No phase beyond the first assumes it will proceed.

Phase 0 · Authority

Confirm who owns each data source and who may decide. Inventory what is available and what restricts it. Resolve the research and ethics pathway before any retrospective analysis. Define roles, access and purpose. Ends in a go or no go record.

Phase 1 · Specification

Inspect sample data under approved access. Document quality, time zone, coverage, duplicates and roster timing. Freeze versioned definitions with their numerator, denominator, grain, window and missingness rules. Build simulated fixtures.

Phase 2 · Feasibility

Only after approval, run a bounded retrospective sample. Compare how the same call is represented across each source. Adjudicate event facts independently. Test repeatability, disagreement and coverage. Assess whether the available cohort can calibrate anything, without treating it as clinical validation.

Phase 3 · Proof of concept

If approved, process a narrow feed in research mode and show descriptive and event review views to authorised reviewers. No automated individual alerts and no workplace decisions.

Phase 4 · Decision

Convene every party with authority, review the evidence and the safeguards, and decide whether any expanded or person level function is permissible. This is a decision gate and not an assumed launch.

What counts as success

Traceable lineage from source to output, reproducible calculations, documented missingness and conflicts, passing role and access tests, reviewed comprehension, and written approval at each gate.

What does not

No clinical benefit, no prediction of self harm and no validated risk classification is a success claim in this scope. The measure describes exposure already carried. It does not forecast anything.

09 · Limits and open questions

What we cannot yet answer.

Who reviews the list

Unassigned. The list stays marked unreviewed until a clinical reviewer reads every term against its class, and no figure is produced from it in the meantime.

Whether a published list changes the radio

Open, and it matters more than it looks. Several terms are safety critical transmissions. If publishing the list made anyone hesitate before saying one of them on the air, that would be a harm this project caused, and it would outweigh the measure. The list is published because a member is owed sight of what could be read about them, and because a measure built on a hidden vocabulary is worse. The question stays open and is addressed to the people who run the calls.

Whether a transcript can support any of this

Unestablished. No source in the set concerns language in radio traffic. Until that gap is closed by evidence rather than by argument, a match is a candidate for a person to review.

Any threshold at all

There is none. A rolling count of events has no validated cutoff and no instrument behind it, and no band, colour or status is assigned to a person anywhere in this system today.

Which codes a department uses

Unconfirmed. Until a department publishes its own signal list, every dialect term stays off.

Who may read a member's figure

Settled, and it is not this site. A member's own figure is read by the member and the body that represents them. An employer sees aggregates. This page holds no member figure, no incident and no record of any kind, and the public site has no login on any page for one to sit behind.

Correction path

If a term on this page is wrong for your department, if a source is misread, or if a limitation is stated too weakly, write to michael.harvey@emr-inc.net and it will be corrected here with the date of the change. The term list and this page both carry a version and a hash so that a correction is visible rather than silent.
