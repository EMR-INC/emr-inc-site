Inside Expect Victims  ·  Blue Team  ·  OHPAH

Three metrics.

Trademarked  ·  Patent pending

OHPAH lives inside Expect Victims. It is not a separate product and it is not a separate login. It is Blue Team, the stats and metrics layer of the platform: the log and the leaderboard, alongside the tool.

Every figure is built from dispatch records the agency already generates. Nothing here is recalled, estimated by the member, or entered after the fact.

Fire Exposure Score Detail

Dispatch classification over a rolling window, joined to the construction era of the dispatched address through the county property appraiser.

Sleep Debt Index Detail

Overnight incidents, fragmented pairs, and the longest unbroken gap in the window.

Work Intensity Index Detail

Dispatch density by time of day. Raw total, plus a rate normalized by rostered units and days in the window.

These are counts of observed dispatch activity. They are not a clinical finding, they are not a diagnosis, and they are not a forecast. The member owns the record and can export it. Departments see aggregates only.

Our position

Open data builds safer communities.

Not as a slogan. As a mechanism. Open records are what let an outside developer build a tool, let a researcher check a claim, and let a member of the public see how their own department is being run. Close the records and all three stop at once.

01

Open by default

Dispatch and incident data should be published in a documented, machine readable form, on a schedule, without a records request and without a fee. It was paid for already.

02

Encryption should be narrow

Encrypt what genuinely warrants it: tactical traffic, patient identifiers, officer safety. Blanket encryption of routine dispatch traffic removes public oversight and returns nothing in exchange.

03

No single gatekeeper

Taxpayer funded data held behind one commercial gate, licensed back to the agency that produced it, has stopped being public data. Centralized control is the same loss as a lock, arriving more politely.

This is the company position, argued in public. It is not a legal conclusion and it is not a research finding.
