01

Source corpus

Server-side structured rows from the live OHPAH Research dispatch corpus. The public response contains aggregates only.

02

Candidate rule

A row counts only when it contains both a recognized apparatus identifier and a broad fire, medical, or motor-vehicle type.

03

Privacy boundary

The query never selects transcript, address, row identifier, audio, or person-level fields; only aggregate counts leave the worker.

04

Publish check

The endpoint and website publisher fail closed when the source is unavailable, no candidates are found, required fields are missing, or totals do not reconcile.

Limits

  • Florida radio systems represented in the OHPAH Research corpus only.
  • Transcript-derived alert candidates are not verified CAD incidents or confirmed unit responses.
  • Repeated transmissions with the same talkgroup, unit, and broad type are grouped within a ten-minute window.
  • No transcript, address, source-row link, row identifier, or person-level record is published.
